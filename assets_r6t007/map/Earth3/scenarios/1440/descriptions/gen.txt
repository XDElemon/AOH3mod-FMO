Geneva, a city-state nestled at the confluence of the Rhône and Arve rivers, is a vital trading hub. Its strategic location has fostered a vibrant economy and a strong sense of independence. Surrounded by powerful neighbors, Geneva must navigate a delicate balance of diplomacy and military preparedness to protect its sovereignty. While the city is renowned for its watchmaking and other crafts, its political future is uncertain as it faces pressures from both the Duchy of Savoy and the Swiss Confederation.
日内瓦是一个坐落在罗讷河和阿尔韦河交汇处的城市国家，是一个重要的贸易中心。其战略位置培育了充满活力的经济和强烈的独立感。日内瓦被强大的邻国包围，必须在外交和军事之间保持微妙的平衡，以保护其主权。虽然这座城市以制表业和其他手工艺品而闻名，但它的政治未来是不确定的，因为它面临着来自萨伏伊公国和瑞士联邦的压力。
